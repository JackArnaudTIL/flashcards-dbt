Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This privacy policy explains how Flashcards ("the app") collects, uses, and stores information when you use the service at jackarnaud.github.io/flashcards-dbt.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive the following information from your Google account:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google profile photo

We also store your study activity within the app:

  • Which flashcards you have attempted
  • How you rated each card (Got it / OK / Hard)

How we use this information

Your name and photo are displayed in the app so you can confirm which account is signed in. Your email is used solely to identify your account.

Your study history is saved so that your progress is preserved across sessions and devices.

We do not use your information for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

How information is stored

Study progress is stored in two places:

  • Locally in your browser's localStorage when you are not signed in
  • Google Firebase Firestore when you are signed in, under your unique user ID

Firebase Firestore is a Google Cloud service. Data stored there is subject to Google's privacy and security practices. Security rules ensure that only you can read or write your own data.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties. The only external service used is Google Firebase, which stores your data on your behalf.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains stored in Firestore for as long as you use the app. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. Alternatively, you can clear your local progress by signing out and clearing your browser's site data.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
For any questions or data deletion requests, email jackarnaudtester@gmail.com.
